# AI Commercial Generator – Make Commercial Videos Online

OpenArt's AI commercial generator lets you create a complete video commercial from a product image, product description, script, or creative idea. Build the ad concept, add your hook and voiceover, and export a version sized for every platform you run.

## Key Features

### Start From A Brief Or A Script

Paste the creative brief you already wrote, or drop in a finished commercial script in any language. The AI commercial maker builds the scenes around whichever one you have.

### Make Any Style Of Commercial

Cinematic, luxury, ecommerce, TVC, lifestyle, food, beauty, and tech product commercials all come out of the same tool. Name the style in your brief and OpenArt matches the look, the pacing, and the lighting to it.

### Multi-Platform Export

Export versions sized for every platform: vertical 9:16 (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), horizontal 16:9 (YouTube/TV), square 1:1 (Instagram feed).

### Built-in Voiceover

Add your hook and voiceover directly in the tool. The audio stays in sync with the generated scenes.

## Workflow

1. **Input**: Product image / description / script / creative brief (any language)
2. **Style**: Specify commercial style (cinematic/luxury/ecommerce/TVC/lifestyle/food/beauty/tech)
3. **Generate**: AI builds scenes matching the style, pacing, and lighting
4. **Voiceover**: Add hook and voiceover, synced to scenes
5. **Export**: Multi-platform sizes (9:16 / 16:9 / 1:1)
