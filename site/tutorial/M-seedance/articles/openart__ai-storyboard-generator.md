# AI Storyboard Generator — Turn a Script into Visual Panels

OpenArt's AI storyboard generator is built for filmmakers, video teams, and marketers who need to see the shot sequence before committing budget to it. Paste a script, shot list, or rough idea and get consistent visual panels back in minutes, no drawing skills required.

## How to Create a Storyboard with AI

### Step 1: Write a prompt for each shot

Open the AI Image Generator and describe the scene, action, and camera angle for one panel at a time. Add your cast from Character Builder so the same faces and settings carry across every panel.

### Step 2: Generate and optimize each panel

Generate from your prompt, then adjust wording and regenerate until the framing and action match your shot list. Fix one panel at a time without affecting others.

### Step 3: Review the full sequence

Preview camera angles, blocking, and pacing before you shoot, pitch a client, or hand the sequence to production.

## Key Features

- **Character Builder**: Same faces and settings across every panel
- **One panel at a time**: Adjust individual shots without regenerating the whole sequence
- **No drawing skills required**: Paste a script or rough idea, get visual panels
- **Preview before production**: See the shot sequence before committing budget

## Use Cases

- **Filmmakers**: Storyboard short films before production
- **Video teams**: Plan shot lists and visual sequences
- **Marketers**: Pitch video concepts to clients
