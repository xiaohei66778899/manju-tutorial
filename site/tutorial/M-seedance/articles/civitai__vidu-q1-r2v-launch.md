# Vidu Q1 Reference to Video (R2V) Launch + New Pricing

> 注：本文正文为客户端渲染（Next.js SSR），通过HTTP直接请求只能获取页面元数据和搜索摘要。以下内容来自页面meta description和搜索索引摘要。

## 文章概述

Today we're excited to announce the official launch of Vidu's new Q1 Reference to Video (R2V) model on Civitai along with lower pricing for Text to Video (T2V) and Image to Video (I2V) Q1 video generation.

## Vidu Q1 R2V 使用方法

1. Go to the Generate page on Civitai
2. Click the "Video" tab
3. Pick "Vidu Q1" from the dropdown
4. Click "Reference to Video"
5. Click "Images" and upload up to 7 reference images
6. Add a prompt (optional)
7. Click Create!

**提示技巧**: If you refer to the images by order uploaded (image1, image2, image3, etc.), you can guide the video's content and style more precisely.

## 三种生成模式

- **Text to Video (T2V)**: Generate from a text prompt
- **Image to Video (I2V)**: Generate from a single image (starting image)
- **Reference to Video (R2V)**: Generate from up to 7 reference images, with text prompt guidance

## 定价更新

同时宣布了T2V和I2V的Q1视频生成新定价（降价）。
