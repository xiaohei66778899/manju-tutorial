F-5 · 动作提示词怎么写

1. 5 字段结构

字段作用学员用
subject主体100%
action动作100%
camera运镜90%
speed速度80%
eyes眼神跟随70%

2. 学员实战模板

subject: 1girl smiling
action: slowly raising right hand
camera: slow push-in
speed: gentle slow motion
eyes: eyes follow the camera

3. 动作词速查

动作英文
转头head turn, looking back
抬手raising hand
走路walking
跑步running
点头nodding
摇头shaking head
笑smiling, laughing
哭crying

4. 运镜词速查

  • slow push-in:慢推
  • slow zoom out:慢拉
  • pan left / right:横移
  • tilt up / down:上下摇
  • orbit:环绕
  • static camera:静止

5. 眼神词(学员运镜规则)

  • eyes follow the camera:眼睛跟随镜头
  • eye contact with viewer:看观众
  • looking up / down:看上下
  • looking away:看向别处
❌

玉哥新规:运镜时眼神必须跟随,否则会"定格感"。

6. 速度词

  • slow motion:慢动作
  • normal speed:正常
  • fast motion:快动作
  • timelapse:延时

7. 学员实战案例(3 个)

案例 1:女主惊讶

subject: 1girl
action: eyes wide open, mouth slightly open
camera: static camera
eyes: looking at viewer
speed: normal

案例 2:反派出场

subject: 1man in black coat
action: slowly stepping forward
camera: low angle shot, slow push-in
eyes: eyes follow the camera
speed: slow dramatic

案例 3:奔跑

subject: 1girl
action: running forward with hair flying
camera: tracking shot, side view
speed: fast motion
eyes: eyes looking forward

8. 数字时间

操作耗时
写 5 字段提示词2-5 分钟
复盘改写5-10 分钟

9. 学完能做啥

  • 能写 5 字段动作提示词
  • 会用眼神跟随
  • 会 3 个玉哥案例
