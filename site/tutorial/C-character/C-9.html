C-9 · 制作表情图

1. 6 大表情

表情英文用在哪
喜happy, smiling小爽点
怒angry, furious冲突爆发
哀sad, sorrowful情感戏
乐laughing, joyful轻喜剧
惊surprised, shocked反转
惧scared, fearful决战前

2. 表情资源分配

角色表情数必备
主角6全套
女主4喜/哀/惊/乐
兄弟2怒/乐
反派 BOSS3怒/惊/冷笑
路人1中性

3. prompt 模板

# 主角"怒"表情
the same man as in the reference, angry expression,
furrowed eyebrows, clenched jaw, intense stare,
white shirt and black pants, medium shot,
white background, 9:16 aspect ratio

# 主角"笑"表情
the same man as in the reference, happy smile,
slight upturn of lips, bright eyes,
white shirt and black pants, medium shot,
white background, 9:16 aspect ratio

📌 关键:用 "the same man as in the reference" + IP-Adapter 锁定角色

4. character expression sheet

# 一键出 6 表情(单图)
character expression sheet, multiple expressions of the same man,
[happy, angry, sad, laughing, surprised, scared],
30 year old Asian man with square face, short black hair,
white shirt, white background, even lighting

📌 学员 50% 用 character expression sheet 一键出

5. 学完这能做啥

  • 能出 6 大表情
  • 会用 expression sheet workflow
  • 能用 IP-Adapter 锁脸表情

6. 小结

📢

📢:
1. 主角 6 表情 · 全套
2. 表情 sheet 一键出 · 学员 50%
3. "the same man as in the reference" · 锁脸
4. 反派 3 表情 · 怒 / 惊 / 冷笑
